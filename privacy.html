Privacy Policy

Pivot keeps your workouts private. It doesn’t collect, sell, or share any of your data.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your workouts, exercises, plan, and settings are stored on your device. Pivot has no accounts, ads, or analytics, and the developer can’t see your data.

If you use iCloud Backup, your Pivot data is included in your device’s backup, like any other app’s.

Apple Health

Pivot only uses Apple Health if you connect it, and asks for permission first. It reads your workouts (type, time, duration, distance, and active calories) and heart rate during them, to show your cardio and its charts. It writes your finished lifting sessions and the cardio you add by hand, so they count toward your Activity rings.

Health data stays on your device. Pivot never sends it to iCloud or anywhere else, and never uses it for advertising. Heart rate is read from Health when you open a workout and is never stored by Pivot. Each device reads its own Health data.

To stop, turn off Pivot’s access in the Health app under Sharing › Apps. If you delete a workout you added in Pivot, Pivot removes its copy from Health too.

iCloud Sync

If you’re signed in to iCloud, Pivot syncs the things you type in, your lifting sessions, exercises, goals, plan, and settings, through your private iCloud account, so they’re the same on your iPhone and iPad. Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy, and the developer can’t see it. Apple Health data is never synced this way.

To stop syncing, turn off Pivot in your iCloud settings. Your data stays on your device.

Reminders

If you set workout reminders or the missed-day nudge, Pivot asks for permission to send notifications first. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent to a server.

Suggestions

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Pivot words its progress suggestions with Apple’s on-device model. Your data never leaves your device for this.

Widgets and the Rest Timer

Pivot’s widgets, Control Center button, and Lock Screen rest timer read today’s plan and your workout from your device. They don’t send your data anywhere.

Contacting Support

If you email support, the message is sent from your own email account. It includes the app version, iOS version, and device model, which you can see before you send it. Your email is used only to answer you.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on the Pivot website with a new date.
