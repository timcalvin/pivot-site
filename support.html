Support

Have a question, found a bug, or have an idea for Pivot? Get in touch.

The easiest way is from the app: tap the gear on any tab, then Contact Support. Your email will include the app version, iOS version, and device model, which helps with answering.

Or email justincamelcasedev@gmail.com with “Pivot Support” in the subject.

Cardio isn’t showing up

Open the Health app, go to Sharing › Apps › Pivot, and make sure Workouts and Heart Rate are turned on. Then open Pivot again.

My lifts aren’t on my other device

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud is on for Pivot in Settings › your name › iCloud. Sync can take a few minutes. Cardio from Apple Health doesn’t sync through Pivot; each device reads it from its own Health app.
