About Pivot: Train & Track

Pivot is a free workout log for iPhone and iPad that tracks cardio and lifting, charts both, and nudges you to show up. Nothing more.

It’s free for good: no ads, no subscription, no account, and no tracking.

What You Can Do

Log a lifting session set by set. Pivot fills in your weight and reps from last time, and a rest timer starts after every set, right on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Pick a goal for each exercise, like getting stronger or building muscle, and Pivot tells you when it’s time to add weight, reps, or a set.

See your cardio from Apple Health without lifting a finger: walks, runs, rides, and swims recorded on Apple Watch, in Fitness, or by apps like Strava. Add one by hand in seconds.

Watch your progress on charts: a mini chart for every exercise, your full history, active minutes per week, and heart rate for each workout.

Plan your week or a repeating cycle, get a reminder on workout days, and skip it automatically when you’ve already trained.

Add a widget for today’s plan, start a workout from Control Center, and pick from five free color themes in light or dark mode.

Your lifts, exercises, and plan sync with iCloud, so they’re the same on your iPhone and iPad.
